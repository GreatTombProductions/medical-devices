About this data: Reports come from the FDA's MAUDE database (Manufacturer and User Facility Device Experience). A report does not mean the device caused harm — it means someone filed a report. Reporting rates vary by device type, manufacturer, and time period. Use this for awareness, not as medical advice. Always consult healthcare professionals about device safety.
